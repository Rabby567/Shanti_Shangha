STEP 14 — Gmail SMTP + Location Map

1. Import database/step14_smtp_location.sql in phpMyAdmin.
2. Admin Dashboard → Settings → Contact & Social → Gmail SMTP.
3. Turn Gmail SMTP ON.
4. Enter the Gmail account that will send mail and its Google App Password.
5. Keep SMTP Host smtp.gmail.com, Port 587, Encryption TLS.
6. The Contact Email/recipient can be a different Gmail address.
7. Admin Dashboard → Settings → Location Map → enter the location/address and save.
8. The footer will show the Google map on the right side.

IMPORTANT: Use a Google App Password, not your normal Gmail password. App Passwords require 2-Step Verification on the Google account.

If you change the SMTP Gmail account, enter a new App Password for that account. The Contact Email recipient can remain different.
